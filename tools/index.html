Time now

Live clocks. Time zones come from your browser's IANA database, so daylight saving time is handled automatically.

ZoneDateTimeAbbr / offsetDST

Most of Arizona (America/Phoenix) stays on MST (UTC−07:00) all year with no daylight saving time. In summer it matches Pacific time; in winter it matches Mountain time. The Navajo Nation in Arizona does observe DST.

Next change = the next time that zone's UTC offset changes, found by scanning ahead in your browser's time zone data (not a fixed list).

Time zone converter

Days move the calendar date and keep the same clock time in the "from" zone. Hours and minutes are exact elapsed time, so adding 24 h across a DST change lands one clock hour off from adding 1 day.
