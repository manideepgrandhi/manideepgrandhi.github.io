Sticky Pad

Quick sticky notes, saved in this browser and working offline. Optionally sync them across devices through your own Google Drive.

Ctrl/⌘+Enter to save · / to start typing
Sync off
Sync across devices with Google Drive

Optional. Notes are kept in this browser and copied to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (scope drive.appdata: this page can see only its own file, not your other Drive files). There is no server; the page talks to Google directly. Connect on each device with the same Google account. Changes sync about 3 seconds after you stop typing, when you come back to the tab, and when you go back online. The newest change to each note wins, and deletes sync too (kept as markers for 30 days).

The sign-in lasts about an hour. After that, auto-sync may need one tap on Sync now, because browsers block sign-in pop-ups that you didn't click.

Not set up yet: this page needs a Google OAuth client ID in sticky/config.js. The steps are in the README.

Notes are stored in this browser's local storage. Unless Google Drive sync is connected, they stay on this device only and clearing site data removes them, so use Export to keep a backup. Shortcuts while editing: Ctrl/⌘+Enter save, Esc cancel. Double-click a note to edit it.
