You may encounter this message when connecting to an instance remotely.  If you log on to the system, it will connect as expected.

To resolve, you can look at a good server's SPN's and see what is different and remove the bad SPN entries.

At Comcast, we do not specifically put in SPN's correlating to the service account.

1. Look at the SPN of a known working system.  In this example, I used DCMDB-WC-1P

a. Open a command prompt

b. Enter: setspn -l DCMDB-WC-1P

i. That is -L with a lowercase L

c. The output should look like this:

  Registered ServicePrincipalNames for CN=DCMDB-WC-1P,OU=DCM Integration,OU=Produc
  tion Databases,OU=Servers,OU=Corporate,DC=cable,DC=comcast,DC=com:

        WSMAN/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com

        WSMAN/DCMDB-WC-1P

        TERMSRV/DCMDB-WC-1P

        TERMSRV/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com

        RestrictedKrbHost/DCMDB-WC-1P

        HOST/DCMDB-WC-1P

        RestrictedKrbHost/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com

        HOST/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com

2. Look at the SPN of the bad system.  In this example, TDSETH-QC-1Q.

a. Open a command prompt or use the same from earlier

b. Enter: setspn -l TDSETH-QC-1Q

i. That is -L with a lowercase L

c. Output may look like this:

  Registered ServicePrincipalNames for CN=tdseth-qc-1q,OU=PENTAHO (TDS),OU=Non-Pro
  duction Databases,OU=Servers,OU=Corporate,DC=cable,DC=comcast,DC=com:

        MSSQLSvc/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com:1433

        MSSQLSvc/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

        TERMSRV/TDSETH-QC-1Q

        TERMSRV/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

        RestrictedKrbHost/TDSETH-QC-1Q

        HOST/TDSETH-QC-1Q

        RestrictedKrbHost/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

        HOST/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

3. Compare the two to see what is on the "bad" spn list that isn't on the good SPN list and vice-versa.  Table for comparison:

  DCMDB-WC-1P (good) TDSETH-QC-1Q (bad)

WSMAN/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com

WSMAN/DCMDB-WC-1P

MSSQLSvc/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com:1433

MSSQLSvc/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

TERMSRV/DCMDB-WC-1P TERMSRV/TDSETH-QC-1Q

TERMSRV/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com TERMSRV/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

RestrictedKrbHost/DCMDB-WC-1P RestrictedKrbHost/TDSETH-QC-1Q

HOST/DCMDB-WC-1P HOST/TDSETH-QC-1Q

RestrictedKrbHost/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com RestrictedKrbHost/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

HOST/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com HOST/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

4. Delete the MSSQLSvc items that exist on the bad host, but not on the good. 

a. setspn -D MSSQLSvc/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com:1433 TDSETH-QC-1Q

b. setspn -D MSSQLSvc/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com TDSETH-QC-1Q

c. setspn -A WSMAN/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com TDSETH-QC-1Q

d. setspn -A WSMAN/TDSETH-QC-1Q TDSETH-QC-1Q

5. Re-run setspn -l for each host and compare the results again.

  DCMDB-WC-1P (good) TDSETH-QC-1Q (should now be good)

WSMAN/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com WSMAN/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

WSMAN/DCMDB-WC-1P WSMAN/TDSETH-QC-1Q

TERMSRV/DCMDB-WC-1P TERMSRV/TDSETH-QC-1Q

TERMSRV/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com TERMSRV/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

RestrictedKrbHost/DCMDB-WC-1P RestrictedKrbHost/TDSETH-QC-1Q

HOST/DCMDB-WC-1P HOST/TDSETH-QC-1Q

RestrictedKrbHost/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com RestrictedKrbHost/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

HOST/DCMDB-WC-1P.cable.comcast.com HOST/TDSETH-QC-1Q.cable.comcast.com

6. Wait approximately 5-10 minutes for the updates to propagate through active directory, and then try to connect to SQL Server remotely.  If still failing, escalate to Comcast L2.
